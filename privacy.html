Privacy policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Who is responsible

ClearBudget is operated by Dan W. Questions and requests about personal information can be sent to lannal0405@gmail.com.

Information used by the desktop app

ClearBudget processes information you enter or import: transaction dates, descriptions, merchants, amounts, currencies, account labels, categories, budgets, import evidence and preferences. Statements and receipts you select may contain additional personal information.

Purpose and storage

This information is used to organize transactions, detect possible duplicate imports and calculate budgets. The current desktop app stores budget records and backups on your computer. It processes statement files and OCR locally. Exported backups are saved where you choose; you control any copies you share or upload. ClearBudget does not operate a hosted budget-data service in the current version.

Bank connections and Enable Banking

The current desktop version does not yet connect to banks through Enable Banking. Registering an application with Enable Banking does not itself give ClearBudget access to a bank account. Before a bank-connection feature is enabled, this notice must identify the account data accessed, permissions, purpose, storage locations, service providers, retention and how access can be revoked. Any connection must require your explicit authorization through the relevant bank and provider.

Optional transaction capture

The optional Apple Pay Shortcuts receiver is disabled by default. When configured, it receives transaction information through the HTTPS route you set up and stores records in the same local ledger. Any proxy or network service you choose may also process connection data under its own policy. ClearBudget does not request bank passwords, Apple passwords or full payment-card numbers for this feature.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them. Removing a transaction within the app preserves its record and evidence for recovery; it is not permanent erasure. To erase the complete local budget, close the app and delete its budget-data file, backup files and exported copies. Original statements and receipts must be deleted separately. Uninstalling the app may leave its data folder behind.

Security and your choices

You control the files you import and export. Protect your computer, account and backups with appropriate access controls. The app's local JSON budget files are not an encrypted vault. You can inspect and correct transactions and export your records. Contact the operator about any information separately held by them, or to raise a privacy concern.

This policy website

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, forms or bank-connection functionality. When hosted on GitHub Pages, the hosting provider logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

The policy will be updated when the application's data handling changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
